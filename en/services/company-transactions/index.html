Quick Questions
Can AIBSHER TAMM process any transaction?

We review each request initially to determine whether it falls within our service scope. Some transactions may require the applicant to attend personally, obtain additional approvals, or deal directly with the relevant authority.

Does AIBSHER TAMM guarantee transaction approval?

No. The final decision belongs to the relevant government or competent authority. Our role is to help you understand requirements, prepare the file, and follow up the steps within scope.
