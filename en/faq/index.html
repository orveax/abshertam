1. About AIBSHER TAMM

1. What is AIBSHER TAMM?

AIBSHER TAMM FOR TRANSACTIONS AND SERVICES is a privately owned and licensed Qatari company that helps customers with a specific transaction understand requirements, prepare the request, and follow up the relevant steps within the service scope.

2. Is AIBSHER TAMM a government entity?

No. AIBSHER TAMM is a private company, not a government entity, and does not represent any ministry or government institution unless a specific official authorization is clearly announced.

3. Who can use AIBSHER TAMM services?

Individuals, business owners, investors, and companies can use AIBSHER TAMM when they have a specific transaction that needs preparation, requirements clarification, coordination, or follow-up. Customer type does not determine service priority; the transaction and current need are the starting point.

2. Services and Transactions

4. What services does AIBSHER TAMM provide?

Our launch services are organized into four Master Services:

  • Company Setup, Renewal, Changes & Licensing
  • Work, Residence & Visa Transactions
  • Attestation, Notary & Official Documents
  • Government Certificates & Selected Records

These contain 10 approved launch paths. Other requests are reviewed before they are treated as available within scope.

5. Can AIBSHER TAMM process any transaction?

We review each request initially to determine whether it falls within our service scope. Some transactions may require the applicant to attend personally, obtain additional approvals, or deal directly with the relevant authority.

6. Does AIBSHER TAMM guarantee transaction approval?

No transaction can be guaranteed because the final decision belongs to the relevant government or competent authority. AIBSHER TAMM's role is to help you understand requirements, review and prepare the file, and follow up the steps within the agreed service scope.

3. Submitting Requests and Documents

7. How do I start a service request?

Use Start Your Request at abshertam.qa/connect and choose the transaction or path closest to your need. If you are unsure, select Not Sure and the request will be reviewed. WhatsApp remains available for enquiry, clarification, and follow-up, but it is not a separate request journey.

8. What information is required when submitting a request?

We start with the minimum needed to route the request:

  • Requested transaction or service
  • The outcome you need
  • Whether you have already started any step
  • Name and phone number
  • Available documents or information when relevant

Additional questions appear only when they affect requirements or routing.

9. Can I send documents through the website?

Required documents may be uploaded through the website when this feature is available, or sent through the official channel specified by the team. Please do not send passwords or confidential banking information.

4. Appointments, Fees, and Execution

10. Do I need an appointment?

It depends on the transaction. Some requests can be reviewed remotely, while others may require an appointment or an in-person visit to the unit.

11. Does submitting an appointment request mean the appointment is confirmed?

No. Submitting the request only means it has been received for review. The team will contact you after reviewing the information to confirm the appointment or request additional details.

12. How are service fees determined?

Fees are determined after identifying the transaction type, scope of work, and requirements. AIBSHER TAMM service fees and any government fees or external costs are explained separately before execution begins.

5. Follow-up and Responsibilities

13. How long does a transaction take?

Timing varies depending on the transaction type, document completeness, and the procedures of the relevant authority. Any timeframe provided is an estimate and not a guarantee of completion by a specific date.

14. How do I follow up my request?

Once a structured request exists, follow-up stays linked to the same customer and request record. Keep the Request ID if one is provided and use the communication channel specified by the team — typically WhatsApp — for updates and the next step without creating a duplicate request.

15. What happens if documents are incomplete or the transaction is rejected?

You will be informed of any required documents or changes when the relevant information is available. If the competent authority rejects the transaction, the team can explain known comments and available next steps, but the final approval or rejection decision remains with the relevant authority.

Did not find your answer?
