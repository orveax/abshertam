01

1. When This Privacy Policy Applies

AIBSHER TAMM FOR TRANSACTIONS AND SERVICES respects the privacy of customers and website visitors and is committed to handling personal data responsibly, transparently, and within the scope of its services and the applicable laws of the State of Qatar.

This Policy applies to data collected through:

  • The website.
  • Appointment booking forms.
  • WhatsApp and telephone communications.
  • Email.
  • Direct visits to the Mobile Service Hub.
02

2. What Data We May Ask You to Provide

Depending on the communication channel and service purpose, we may collect:

  • Name.
  • Phone number.
  • WhatsApp number.
  • Customer type: company, individual, or investor.
  • Company name where relevant.
  • Service category or purpose of the appointment.
  • Preferred appointment date or time.
  • Any additional information voluntarily provided by the customer.
  • Documents voluntarily uploaded when document upload is enabled, or later sent through the designated service email.

We do not request highly sensitive documents unless they are genuinely required and the customer has been informed of the reason and the appropriate submission channel.

03

3. How We Use Data

We use personal data to:

  • Receive and review appointment requests.
  • Contact the customer to confirm or adjust an appointment.
  • Understand the initial need and direct the customer to the appropriate service.
  • Create an internal follow-up record or CRM lead.
  • Manage communications through phone, WhatsApp, or email.
  • Improve the website and service experience.
  • Meet applicable legal or regulatory requirements where necessary.

The website is primarily intended to present the company and facilitate appointment booking. Submitting the form does not automatically commence service execution.

04

4. How We Use Communication and Request-Management Systems

Contact details and a summary of the request or conversation may be recorded in a CRM or internal follow-up tool to organize appointments, communications, and customer service.

Technical services such as hosting, email, digital forms, WhatsApp Business, and analytics may be used to operate the website and manage communication. This does not mean that customer data is sold or used for purposes unrelated to the service.

05

5. When Your Data May Be Shared

AIBSHER TAMM does not sell, rent, or trade customer data for commercial or marketing purposes.

Data may be made available only as necessary to:

  • Authorized AIBSHER TAMM personnel.
  • Technical service providers supporting the website, email, CRM, or forms.
  • Relevant authorities where required by law.
  • Licensed service partners where their involvement is necessary and the customer is informed when appropriate.
06

6. Marketing Consent

Customer data will not be used for marketing communications unless the customer provides separate, optional consent.

Declining marketing consent does not affect appointment booking or service access.

07

7. Cookies and Analytics Tools

The website may use basic cookies and analytics technologies to understand usage and improve performance and user experience.

Usage data may be analyzed in aggregated or anonymized form, such as pages visited or buttons clicked, without being used to directly identify the customer.

Users may control cookies through their browser settings where available.

08

8. How We Protect Your Data and Documents

AIBSHER TAMM applies appropriate administrative and technical safeguards to protect personal data from unauthorized access, use, alteration, or disclosure.

No electronic method can guarantee absolute security. Customers should therefore avoid sending sensitive information or documents through channels not designated for that purpose.

09

9. Data Retention

AIBSHER TAMM retains personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected, manage the customer relationship, and meet relevant legal or administrative requirements.

Data is deleted or anonymized when there is no longer a legitimate need to retain it, unless the law or nature of the service requires a longer period.

10

10. Your Data Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law, customers may request to:

  • Know what personal data is held about them.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Update contact information.
  • Withdraw marketing consent.
  • Request deletion or restriction where permitted.
  • Ask how their data is processed.

12. Third-Party Links and Websites

The website may contain links to third-party platforms or websites. AIBSHER TAMM does not control their privacy practices, and users should review their policies separately.

13

13. Updates to This Privacy Policy

This Policy may be updated when services, legal requirements, or operating tools change. The latest update date will be displayed at the top of the page. Continued use after an update constitutes acknowledgment of the revised Policy.

14

14. Governing Version and Language

This Policy is available in Arabic and English. In the event of any inconsistency in interpretation, the Arabic version will prevail unless otherwise required by law.

15
