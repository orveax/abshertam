## Decision
Round 1.6 is a refinement and responsive rebuild. It does not reopen the locked M1 structural order or change the five-stage public journey or six-step `/connect` flow.

## Implemented changes

### Homepage
- Preserved the approved desktop composition and section order.
- Rebuilt the five-stage Public Journey as a connected path with a clear start, end, and CTA handoff.
- Rebuilt service cards so each expanded state contains description, examples, a primary category-preselected `/connect` CTA, and a category-aware WhatsApp CTA.
- Retained a compact mobile accordion state.
- Applied one unified local SVG line-icon system.
- Preserved the Mobile Service Truck as a structural wireframe visual.
- Preserved dynamic Doha unit status and date/time; weather uses a configurable endpoint with a safe fallback.
- Rebuilt the footer as four text groups: Company, Help & Information, Contact, Social.
- Social routes remain hidden until official URLs are configured.
- Company Profile is an external configurable route and remains hidden until an approved URL is provided. No PDF is generated by the site.

### Responsive behavior
- Arabic off-canvas opens from the right.
- English off-canvas opens from the left.
- Support/legal navigation wraps or collapses without horizontal scrolling.
- Tablet journey remains balanced and does not leave the fifth step isolated.
- Mobile journey becomes a vertical connected timeline.
- Mobile service cards use a compact summary and expand for detail/actions.
- Mobile footer groups collapse without hiding required links.

### Support & Legal System
- FAQ, Terms, and Privacy share one shell, cross-navigation, language behavior, menu, final dark support panel, and footer.
- FAQ includes 15 Arabic and 15 English questions in five categories with a single-open accordion.
- Terms includes 21 sections in each language.
- Privacy includes 15 sections in each language.
- Tablet/mobile TOC uses a compact grouped drawer instead of a long internal scrolling sidebar.
- Legal status remains **Legal Review Required**.

### `/connect`
- Preserved six steps and customer-to-service routing.
- Preserved conditional Company Name logic.
- Preserved 15-file / 10 MB each / 40 MB total rules.
- Preserved 06:30–15:30 appointment range in 15-minute increments.
- Preserved support/legal links and session form state.
